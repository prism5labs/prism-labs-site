The pieces, in plain words

A Spark is one button for one job — push it and the job gets done, today and every day. A Plug is a Spark with no button to push — it watches for the moment and does the job by itself. A Satellite is a whole assistant built around one single job, done every day. A Supernova is a whole assistant for an entire front office. Upkeep is a small monthly amount for outright buyers — fixes, upgrades, new jobs, priority help — never required, and already included in every monthly plan.

Four ways to pay: once, and it's yours for good · monthly, with Upkeep included · monthly for 2 years at a lower price · or a year up front, 12 months for the price of 11. The card company adds a 3% processing fee at checkout — so a Supernova bought outright comes to $514 in total.
