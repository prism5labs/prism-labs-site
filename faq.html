Q1What exactly am I getting?+

An AI assistant of your own — a named person on your phone or computer who does real jobs: sorting messages, keeping your calendar, drafting replies, chasing paperwork. You talk to it like a person; it works like staff.

Q2Will it send things without asking me?+

No — everything it writes is a draft until you say go, with no exceptions and no way to switch that off.

Q3How does the free trial work?+

Fourteen days, one real job, on your real work — no card, no charge, no auto-renew, and a reminder on day 11.

Q4What if I stop paying a monthly plan?+

The living service pauses until you start again — nothing is deleted, nobody chases you, and outright buyers are never paused.

Q5Can I change its name or how it talks?+

Any time — name, voice, rules, duties: tell it or tell us, and the change stays changed.
