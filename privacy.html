Privacy policy

What our apps access when you connect an account, how that information is used, and what we never do with it — in plain English.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Who we are

Prism 5 Labs is a small, family-built business in Green Bay, Wisconsin. We make personal AI assistants — the Prism app — and Loremaster, our interactive-story app. When this policy says "we" or "us," it means Prism 5 Labs. You can reach us any time at prism5labscontact@gmail.com or (920) 393-7294.

This policy covers this website and both apps. It does not cover services we link to or that you connect — those have their own policies.

What the apps access when you connect a Google account

Connecting a Google account is always your choice, and the apps work at a basic level without one. If you do connect, Google asks you to approve specific access first, and we only receive what you approve:

  • Sign-in. Your name and email address, so the app knows the account is yours.
  • Gmail. The assistant can read your inbox so it can show and summarize your mail, prepare replies and drafts, and — only when you approve a specific message — send it.
  • Google Calendar. The assistant can read your calendar so it knows your schedule, and create events you ask it to book.
  • Google Drive. The assistant can read files you point it at, so it can work with your own documents.

The apps also keep the things you give them directly: your messages to the assistant, your settings, and — in Loremaster — your place in a story and your saved tales.

If you email us, sign up for our mailing list, or buy something from us, we receive what you send — your email address, your message, and your order details. Payments are handled by our payment processor; we never see or store your full card number.

How your information is used

For one purpose: doing your own work, in your own assistant, at your direction. Reading your mail so it can be summarized. Drafting a reply for your approval. Booking the event you asked for. Picking your story back up where you left it.

Our assistants work drafts-first. Nothing is sent, posted, or booked in your name without your OK on that specific item.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information. To anyone, ever.
  • We never use your private data — your mail, your calendar, your files — for advertising.
  • We never use your private data to train AI models.
  • We never hand your information to third parties except the service providers needed to run the apps (for example, our hosting and payment providers), and they may use it only to provide their service to us.

Google API disclosure

Prism 5 Labs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain terms: information from your Google account is used only to provide and improve the assistant features you asked for, it is not transferred to others except as needed to run those features, and it is never used for advertising.

Storage and security

Information the apps need — your account connection, your conversations with the assistant, your settings — is stored on the servers that run the apps, so your assistant is there when you come back. Connections to your accounts travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the sign-in tokens that keep an account connected are stored on our servers, not on your device.

We are a small business, and we keep this simple and honest: access to stored information is limited to what is needed to run and support the apps, and we do not keep information we do not need.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can disconnect a connected account at any time, right in the app. Disconnecting stops all further access to that account.

You can also ask us to delete your information entirely. Email prism5labscontact@gmail.com and we will delete the account data we hold for you and confirm when it is done. Order records we are required to keep for bookkeeping are kept only as long as the law requires.

Cookies and storage on your device

This website does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. The apps store small amounts of data on your own device — things like your settings, your theme, and your place in a story — so they open fast, remember you, and can work through a patchy connection. That data stays on your device and you can clear it any time through your browser or app settings.

Your account, your responsibility

An account is set up by an adult, and the account holder is responsible for anyone they choose to let use it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say so plainly on this page.
