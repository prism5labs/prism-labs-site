What it does

What a personal assistant does

Plain words, no jargon. If a job is on this list, your assistant does it the way you'd brief a good hire — and shows you the draft before anything leaves the building.

Sorts your inbox

Every message read, sorted, and answered with a draft for your OK — the noise disappears, what needs you surfaces.

Keeps your calendar

Bookings, reminders, reschedules — and a short morning briefing so the day never ambushes you.

Drafts your replies

Check-ins, follow-ups, answers to customers — written in your words, waiting for your tap.

Chases the paperwork

Quotes out the same day, invoices followed up politely, forms copied across without the typos.

Does the research

Questions answered, options compared, summaries in plain words — before you have to ask twice.

Watches what you'd miss

Unknown callers identified, missed calls caught, deadlines watched — nothing falls through the cracks.
